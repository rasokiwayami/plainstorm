## Conceptual inspiration

PlainStorm also uses generic grill, question-routing, and blindspot-review
concepts associated with Matt Pocock's public
[`mattpocock/skills`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/6654f6b60cd9d5be8b54c6fafe44346dabeb3b76)
materials, including the `grill-me` concept, at commit
`6654f6b60cd9d5be8b54c6fafe44346dabeb3b76`. This integration was written
clean-room: it copies no upstream text or source structure, so no additional
Matt Pocock license file is included. The Superpowers derivative remains
covered by its listed MIT notice.
