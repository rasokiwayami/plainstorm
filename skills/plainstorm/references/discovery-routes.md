# Discovery Routes

Use the frame in the entrypoint to select the cheapest route that can change a
material decision. The route is a hypothesis about the blocker and must be
reconsidered when evidence changes it.

Calibrate relevant domain familiarity as known, partial, or new from context
or one decision-changing experience question. This calibration is an internal
input, not a ritual or a visible quota. Inspect relevant context, prior art,
and failure modes; explain concrete blindspots and why they matter, then
reclassify concrete findings into F or U.

## Internal unknown lens

Use the four-way unknown model as an internal discovery lens, not an output
quota: Known Knowns, Known Unknowns, Unknown Knowns, and Unknown Unknowns.
Store verified facts as F, user decisions as D, and unresolved,
decision-relevant items as active **U**. Do not duplicate Known Knowns in U,
and do not emit generic Unknown Unknowns placeholders. Concrete blindspots are
found through relevant context, prior art, and failure modes; reclassify
concrete findings into F or U. Discover tacit criteria from the user's reactions
to references, materially different options, or a prototype, then record the
result as D or active U.

## Route cues

- **Light** synthesizes enough verified context and user decisions to make a
  bounded next step observable. It can finish without asking a question.
- **Deep Grill** handles a material design tree; use the complete independent
  frontier and its completion boundary in [Deep Grill](deep-grill.md).
- **Blindspot/Research** checks factual or unfamiliar-domain gaps using
  relevant sources and observations. Research facts; do not infer a private
  preference or authority from them.
- **Options/Prototype** offers materially different options with
  decision-relevant trade-offs when tacit taste or criteria are cheaper to
  observe than to elicit. Present the route and recommendation, then wait for
  explicit user selection before construction.
- **Evidence-driven re-entry** compares the expected outcome with what a
  material probe or implementation actually found, records the material delta,
  and chooses the next route.

## References are partial specifications

Treat a reference as a partial specification. Identify the aspect it is
authoritative about, extract only relevant semantics, and record explicit
non-goals for what it does not establish rather than cloning the whole
reference or inheriting its unstated assumptions. If the authoritative aspect
is unclear, keep that uncertainty active and ask or research the smallest
decision-changing question.
