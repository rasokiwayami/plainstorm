# PlainStorm

Use PlainStorm as one single adaptive loop when the shape of a decision or
solution is still being discovered. Keep a compact internal frame for:

- purpose
- use context
- problem/value
- observable success
- non-goals/constraints
- largest material uncertainty
- next validation

Do not require all fields to be shown every turn. Ordinary responses expose
only the new, materially changed, or reopened F/U/D state described in
[state and re-entry](references/state-and-reentry.md).

Choose a route by the kind of unresolved blocker, never by a question count:

- **Light**: the work is decision-ready and may finish with zero questions.
- **Deep Grill**: exhaustive grilling is requested, or a material branching,
  high-impact, or hard-to-reverse decision remains.
- **Blindspot/Research**: an unfamiliar or factual gap needs discoverable
  evidence.
- **Options/Prototype**: tacit taste or criteria are cheaper to observe in
  materially different options or a reversible prototype.
- **Evidence-driven re-entry**: prototype or implementation evidence changes
  what the loop needs to resolve.

Use the route rules and the internal four-way discovery lens in
[discovery routes](references/discovery-routes.md), the frontier and completion
boundary in [Deep Grill](references/deep-grill.md), and state, prototype, and
re-entry rules in [state and re-entry](references/state-and-reentry.md).

PlainStorm is not an automatic route for code review, debugging, or
implementation of an already-settled specification. It does not expand
authority or perform external effects; ordinary implementation still follows
the applicable plan, review, and authority boundaries. Do not import a display
ritual or the Unknowns runtime harness.
